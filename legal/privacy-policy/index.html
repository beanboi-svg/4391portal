Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 27, 2026

1. Who We Are

Frederick Theatre Online Portal (“we,” “our,” or “the Portal”) is a website operated for the Theatre Troupe 4391 program at Frederick High School.

Our website address is https://portal.4391.org.

The Portal is used by students, staff, and authorized participants to record and manage work hours related to Frederick High School theatrical productions.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information submitted through the Frederick Theatre Online Portal. It applies to all users who access or use the Portal for any purpose related to Theatre Troupe 4391 activities, including security and abuse-prevention measures.

3. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Your name and grade level, used to accurately identify students and manage user accounts.
  • Activity Information: Records of work hours, including the date and time each entry is submitted.
  • Device and Session Information: Limited technical information such as browser type, device characteristics, IP-related data, session cookies, and interaction data used to support site functionality and protect against automated or malicious activity.

Some device and interaction information may be collected automatically through security services used on the Portal, including Google reCAPTCHA.

We collect this information solely to manage and verify student participation in Frederick High School theatrical productions, maintain system security, and provide a reliable user experience.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Managing Student Participation: To track and verify student work hours for theatrical productions.
  • Analysis and Oversight: Authorized theatre directors and selected students review data to ensure accuracy and maintain program records.
  • Administrative Access: Certain information may be shared with school administrators for official oversight or reporting purposes.
  • Demonstrations and Outreach: Aggregated or anonymized data may be shown to other schools or organizations to demonstrate the Portal’s features.
  • Session and Security Management: To maintain login sessions, prevent unauthorized access, and protect the Portal from spam, abuse, or automated misuse.

We do not use your information for marketing purposes, do not sell user data, and do not share information beyond the uses described in this policy.

5. Data Sharing and Security

Access to personal information is strictly limited to:

  • Authorized theatre directors
  • Approved students involved in program management
  • School administrators for official oversight

The Portal also uses Google reCAPTCHA, a third-party security service operated by Google, to protect against automated abuse. reCAPTCHA processes certain device and interaction data directly on Google’s systems for security purposes. We do not receive or store detailed reCAPTCHA analytics.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Review Your Information: You may review personal identifiers such as your name and grade level.
  • Data Removal: You may request that your information be permanently deleted from the Portal, subject to school recordkeeping requirements.
  • Opting Out: Choosing not to use the Portal means you will not be able to log work hours for theatrical productions.

To exercise these options, contact the theatrical director. Students cannot modify logged work-hour durations, as these records are maintained for program accuracy.

7. Cookies, Tracking, and Security Tools

The Frederick Theatre Online Portal uses cookies and similar technologies solely for essential functionality and security.

  • Session Cookies: Used to maintain your login session and deleted automatically when the session ends.
  • Security Technologies: Google reCAPTCHA may use cookies or identifiers to determine whether activity is legitimate and to prevent abuse.

The Portal does not use cookies or tracking technologies for advertising, marketing, or behavioral analytics.

8. Google reCAPTCHA

This site is protected by Google reCAPTCHA to ensure the security and integrity of user submissions.

reCAPTCHA may collect information such as IP address, device characteristics, mouse movements, keystrokes, and interaction patterns. This information is collected and processed directly by Google and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

We do not store or access detailed reCAPTCHA data beyond confirmation that verification was successful.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in Portal operations, security practices, or legal requirements. When updates are made, the “Last Updated” date will be revised. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
