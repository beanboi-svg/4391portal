Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2025

1. Who We Are

Frederick Theatre Online Portal (“we,” “our,” or “the Portal”) is a website operated for the Theatre Troupe 4391 program at Frederick High School.

Our website address is https://portal.4391.org.

The Portal is used by students, staff, and authorized participants to record and manage work hours related to Frederick High School theatrical productions.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information submitted through the Frederick Theatre Online Portal. It applies to all users who access or use the Portal for any purpose related to Theatre Troupe 4391 activities.

3. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Your name and grade level, which help us accurately identify students and manage user accounts.
  • Activity Information: Records of work hours, including the date and time each entry is submitted.
  • Device and Session Information: Basic device details to support the Portal’s functionality and cookies that maintain your login session while you are using the site.

We collect this information solely to manage and verify student participation in Frederick High School theatrical productions, and to provide a smooth user experience on the Portal.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Managing Student Participation: To track and verify student work hours for theatrical productions.
  • Analysis and Oversight: Authorized theatre directors and selected students review the data to ensure accuracy and maintain program records.
  • Administrative Access: Certain information may be shared with school administrators for official oversight or reporting purposes.
  • Demonstrations and Outreach: Aggregated or anonymized data may be shown to other schools or organizations interested in the Portal to demonstrate its features.
  • Session Management: Cookies used to maintain your login session are automatically deleted after each session ends.

We do not use your information for marketing purposes, sell it, or share it outside of the contexts listed above.

5. Data Sharing and Security

We do not share your personal information with any third parties outside of the uses described above. Access to student data is strictly limited to:

  • Authorized theatre directors
  • Approved students involved in program management
  • School administrators for oversight

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including restricting access to authorized users and maintaining secure login sessions. Cookies used to manage sessions are deleted after each session to enhance security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Review Your Information: You may review your personal identifiers, such as your name and grade level.
  • Data Removal: You may request that your information be permanently deleted from the Portal.
  • Opting Out: Choosing not to use the Portal means you will not be able to log work hours for theatrical productions.

To exercise any of these options, please contact the theatrical director at the time you wish to review, remove, or opt out of your data. Note that students cannot modify logged work-hour durations; this information is maintained for program accuracy.

7. Cookies and Tracking

The Frederick Theatre Online Portal uses cookies solely to maintain session functionality. Cookies help the Portal remember your login while you log your work hours and allow you to return to the site without having to log in again during the same session.

Cookies are deleted automatically once your session ends. We do not use cookies for advertising, analytics, or tracking beyond this essential functionality.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in how the Frederick Theatre Online Portal operates or to comply with applicable laws. When we make updates, we will revise the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy. We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
